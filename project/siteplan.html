NGLANE

Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to give publicity to NGLANE, a fashion brand focused on showcasing ready-to-wear and bespoke clothing. I decided to put the brand on the internet to increase visibility, strengthen brand identity, and reach a wider audience beyond physical locations.

Scenarios

  • What types of clothing does NGLANE offer, and how can I view the available designs?
  • What is the story behind NGLANE, and what makes the brand unique?
  • How can I contact NGLANE for inquiries or custom design requests?
  • How can I sign up to receive updates about new collections and brand news?

The target audience includes individuals of all ages, with a primary focus on young adults and fashion-conscious individuals who actively use the internet and social media. The fashion industry in Nigeria is rapidly growing, as many people are interested in expressing themselves through style and sharing fashion content online. However, many emerging brands still lack professional websites that clearly communicate their identity and showcase their collections effectively. This site will present NGLANE’s designs, brand story, and values in a visually appealing way that encourages visitors to connect with the brand. The website will be accessible on any device with a web browser and internet connection, including desktops, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices.
